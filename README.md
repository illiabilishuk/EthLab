# EthLab

**What is EthLab?**

ethlab.io is a website aimed at simplifying the lives of Web3 developers. It primarily operates on the Viem library, which is basically a coating for ETH calls. (You can familiarize yourself with Viem in detail either by using the official documentation -> https://viem.sh/ or by opening my repo "Viem.ts-Documentation" -> https://github.com/illiabilishuk/Viem.ts-Documentation, where I tried to embed all calls into code so others could adapt the commands more quickly.)

**Why did I build EthLab?**

After I created my copy-and-paste version of the Viem documentation, following my interest in blockchain technology, EthLab served primarily as good practice for using those methods in the real world. But shortly after I started developing the product, I found out that it was very difficult to find any adequate Web3 tools out there. Many of you can give me an example of Etherscan, a wonderful tool with many features and insights. And I will agree with you; however, it serves better for traders and investors and also for blockchain analysts and institutions, lacking in-depth features like block simulations, ABI JSON/Item converters, and message signatures. I created this tool to complement Etherscan, not to replace it. At this stage, Etherscan provides real-time information like gas prices and transactions, while EthLab focuses on overall blockchain analysis, making them complementary tools.

**What does EthLab do?**

As already mentioned, EthLab provides and attempts to integrate heavy-load blockchain analysis into a web browser for simpler and quicker use. I will not be going through all methods in this repo, but you can give them a quick look in the "Docs" section on EthLab -> https://ethlab.io/docs. In summary, EthLab currently offers two distinct sections: methods and converters. Methods are direct eth calls based on the Viem library. The methods section is split into different methods, which are grouped by their purpose for easier navigation. Converters are, as their name suggests, converters that range from simple units to complex JSON formats. Converters are aimed at making developers' lives easier by providing them with the correct type of content they may need while building various dapps and Web3 infrastructures.

**Who is EthLab for?**

EthLab is mainly targeted at Web3 developers, builders, and analysts. However, I am constantly considering new ways in which other Web3 and Web2 occupants can make use of it.

**What is the future of EthLab?**

My number one target at this stage is to make this product as fine and sound as possible. Therefore I encourage everyone who has any ideas or proposals regarding EthLab to leave them in the CONTRIBUTING.md file. Going over my next steps of development, I would like to make the documentation more comprehensible, make EthLab adaptive for tablets, and keep on adding other methods.

**EthLab's commerciality**

EthLab is a fully accessible and free website for anyone in need. However, as EthLab's functionality and client base develop, I may find it beneficial to establish a subscription plan, since I am currently the only person working on EthLab, and additional paid features may be introduced in the future. You can find a "Donate" page in the header on the main page, even though EthLab is not currently commercial. If you wish to donate, simply enter the amount in ETH and connect your wallet to the website. By donating, you are helping to keep the site running and giving me resources for further modifications.
